# AI Workflow — 2026.08.1

## Highlights

A small maintenance release. Runs that pause to ask a human a question now behave correctly in every case, and the release pipeline that delivers updates to your environment was hardened after its first production run.

## What's new

No new user-facing capabilities in this release.

## Improvements and fixes

- Runs waiting for a human answer are shown as "Awaiting input" and reliably pick up again on every path out of that state, whether the question is answered, the ticket moves, or the run is cancelled. Previously a run could occasionally stay stuck after its question was resolved.
  <!-- sources: 200 -->
- The release pipeline handles complex change histories more robustly, so future updates reach your environment without manual intervention.
  <!-- sources: 199 -->

## Do you need to do anything?

No. The update is applied for you.

## Known limitations

No known user-facing limitations.
